    ●   Pride (superbia): An excessive love of self and one's own excellence, viewed as
        the root and "queen" of all sins.
    ●   Greed (avaritia): An excessive attachment to or desire for material wealth and
        possessions.
    ●   Lust (luxuria): A disordered or excessive craving for sexual pleasure.
    ●   Envy (invidia): A selfish sadness or resentment over the good fortune, traits, or
        possessions of others.
    ●   Gluttony (gula): The over-consumption of food, drink, or other resources beyond
        what is needed.
    ●   Wrath / Anger (ira): Uncontrolled rage, a desire for revenge, or an excessive
        reaction against another's will.
    ●   Sloth / Acedia (acedia): A lack of care, spiritual apathy, or laziness regarding
        one's duties and responsibilities

2. Define the seven archetypes

These are the personality foundations your questions will sort people into.

1. Pride — The Sovereign

Driven by self-worth, recognition, autonomy, and personal standards.

    ●   Light: Confidence, leadership, self-respect.
    ●   Shadow: Arrogance, defensiveness, superiority.
    ●   Core fear: Being insignificant or seen as lesser.

2. Envy — The Comparer

Driven by longing, comparison, aspiration, and the desire for what feels out of reach.

    ●   Light: Ambition, observation, inspiration.
    ●   Shadow: Resentment, jealousy, inadequacy.
    ●   Core fear: Being left behind or not having enough.

3. Gluttony — The Indulger

Driven by experience, sensation, consumption, and the desire for more.

    ●   Light: Passion, enthusiasm, generosity, curiosity.
    ●   Shadow: Excess, escapism, overindulgence.
    ●   Core fear: Deprivation, emptiness, missing out.

4. Sloth — The Drifter

Driven by peace, comfort, preservation of energy, and resistance to unnecessary demands.

    ●   Light: Patience, reflection, adaptability.
    ●   Shadow: Avoidance, stagnation, procrastination.
    ●   Core fear: Being overwhelmed or forced beyond capacity.

5. Wrath — The Catalyst

Driven by justice, intensity, control, and the need to respond to what feels wrong.

    ●   Light: Courage, conviction, protection.
    ●   Shadow: Aggression, impulsivity, vengeance.
    ●   Core fear: Helplessness or being violated.
6. Greed — The Collector

Driven by security, ownership, opportunity, and the desire to build or accumulate.

    ●   Light: Resourcefulness, ambition, strategy.
    ●   Shadow: Possessiveness, exploitation, never having enough.
    ●   Core fear: Losing control, security, or what they've earned.

7. Lust — The Enchanter

Driven by connection, desire, intensity, attraction, and emotional or sensory fulfillment.

    ●   Light: Charisma, intimacy, passion, magnetism.
    ●   Shadow: Obsession, temptation, dependency, manipulation.
    ●   Core fear: Disconnection, rejection, or feeling undesirable.
